2. Frameworks

Three consistent ways to value a business. Each treats the benefit of debt financing differently.

APV

Adjusted Present Value

Value the business as if unlevered, then add the value of financing separately.

Read more →

WACC

Weighted Average Cost of Capital

Discount unlevered free cash flows at a blended, after-tax cost of capital.

Read more →

FTE

Flow to Equity

Value equity directly by discounting the cash flows available to shareholders.
